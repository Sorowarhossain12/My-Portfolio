I model how blood and heat move through the body, from coronary bypass grafts to cryosurgery of tumours, using computational fluid dynamics and machine learning.

About

I am a lecturer in applied mathematics at Sonargaon University in Dhaka. My work sits between mathematical modeling and medicine: I build finite volume simulations of blood flow, drug delivery and phase-change heat transfer, and test them against experimental data. I also coordinate Chapal’s Research Lab, where I help other researchers and students carry out their research.

Research interests

  • Heat and mass transfer
  • ML-assisted CFD
  • Medical image segmentation
